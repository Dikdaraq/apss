ABOUT THE SEMINAR

A meeting place for
pharmaceutical research.

The Ardabil Pharmaceutical Sciences Seminar brings together students, researchers, faculty members, and professionals to share new findings, exchange ideas, and encourage collaboration across pharmaceutical disciplines.

Participants are invited to submit an abstract and a scientific poster in accordance with the official submission instructions. Please check the deadlines and prepare both files as PDFs.
